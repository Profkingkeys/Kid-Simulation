# Teaching and review notes

Version 0.2 uses authored, deterministic scenarios. Complete the campaign yourself before inviting learners. Ask them to explain why a rejected option is wrong; points are a navigation aid, not a competence score. Exported attempt reports have no names or patient identifiers. Do not add identifying information to a shared report.

Review content, accessibility and transfer of learning separately. A correct game choice does not demonstrate safe professional practice. Record reviewer name, qualification, jurisdiction, date, scenario version, proposed changes and approval scope before formal use. This repository has not yet received that review.

## Healthy home

The handwashing routine follows the [CDC five-step guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/clean-hands/about/), including at least twenty seconds of scrubbing. The child can fail a choice safely and retry it. Do not shame or frighten learners. Demonstrate the real routine with suitable adult supervision after playing.

Medicine and kitchen scenarios deliberately direct the child to a trusted adult. Water is selected by a known safe source, not its appearance. Assess whether learners can explain these decisions in their own words. Test with keyboard-only access, touch devices and children with varied reading abilities. Spoken narration and translated content are future work, not shipped features.
